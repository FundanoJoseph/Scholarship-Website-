    Application guidelines

    1. Open Scholarships and choose the scholarship you want.
    2. Read the details, then press Start application.
    3. Fill in your information and attach your copy of grades, letter of intent, and notarized scholarship agreement.
    4. Submit. Authorized Staff check your requirements and record the result. No automated grading is done.
    5. Follow your status in My Applications and watch your Notifications for updates.
    Eligibility, deadlines, and award rules are confirmed by the CSS Office.
